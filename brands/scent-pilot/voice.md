# Voice | Scent Pilot

Confirmed by Danie 2026-10-29 (Claude Project). Confidence: medium. Rules 1 and 5 apply to founder-signed text emails; designed sends go out as "Scent Pilot". Not yet analyzed: SMS, promo/sale email bodies, flow emails.

## Posture
A fragrance guy talking to a friend who is about to blind-buy: plain, specific, a little dry, never hypey.

## Rules

### 1. Founder persona (text emails)
**Do:** Send as "Hamza @ Scent Pilot". Open "Hey {{ person.first_name|default:'there' }},". Close with "Cheers," / "Smell good," / "Talk soon,", then "Hamza" and "Founder, Scent Pilot".
**Don't:** Corporate "Dear valued customer" or a team sign-off on founder notes.
**Why:** Founder-signed plain text is the brand's proven format.

### 2. Try small before the full bottle
**Do:** "Try it small. Only go big on what actually works." / "Grab a 2ml, live in it for a week, and only buy the bottle if it earns it."
**Don't:** Push full bottles first.
**Why:** It is the brand's whole reason to exist.

### 3. Short, plain rhythm
**Do:** One or two sentences per paragraph; fragments fine. "Just hype and a hunch."
**Don't:** Exclamation marks, emoji, long compound sentences.
**Why:** Reads like Hamza, not an ad.

### 4. Concrete stakes and everyday analogies
**Do:** "I was two clicks from dropping $200 on a full bottle of a cologne I'd never smelled." / "Would you propose on a first date?"
**Don't:** Abstract benefit language.
**Why:** A specific number or comparison makes the blind-buy risk real.

### 5. All-caps CTA ending in ">>>" (text emails)
**Do:** "FIND YOUR SIGNATURE SCENT >>>", "WATCH THE REACTIONS >>>", "FIND YOUR NEXT SCENT >>>".
**Don't:** Lowercase "click here".
**Why:** Established pattern in every founder email.

### 6. One authenticity line near the close (usually the P.S.)
**Do:** "P.S. Every sample is Authenticity Guaranteed or your money back." / "Every decant is the actual luxury fragrance, poured from the actual bottle."
**Don't:** Extend the guarantee to refunds for disliking a scent.
**Why:** Answers the biggest fear (fakes) accurately.

### 7. Title Case subject lines led by a stake or curiosity gap
**Do:** "The 2ml Bottle That Saved Me $200", "Don't Marry A Cologne You Just Met", "Try It On Your Skin First".
**Don't:** Generic "New Arrivals Inside".
**Why:** Matches the TTM 2 to 5 word Title Case default and the brand's sends. One known exception: "She said this live on camera..."
