## Earlier feedback still relevant (client file, Jun to Sep 2026)
- Remove "freshly decanted" and "sterile equipment" (2026-06-26). "I would avoid the sample is freshly line and that angle; approach from a brand angle, like we value customers" (2026-07-14).
- Too much authenticity focus "comes off like we're hiding something" (2026-07-07).
- Packaging claims make him nervous ("potential exposure") (2026-07-14).
- Don't pit two fragrances against each other as if one is a bad choice; "they can buy both" (2026-07-07).
- Don't take shots at people buying from YouTube reviews: "ironic since I am a YouTuber"; keep the "almost gambling $200" idea (2026-08-30).
- Street interviews are with girls, not guys (2026-08-30).
- Notes must be exact; verify on Fragrantica. LV Imagination: no pear, use black tea and ginger. Dior Homme 2020 is not Dior Homme Intense or the original: top bergamot, pink pepper, elemi; middle Atlas cedar, cashmere wood, patchouli; base Iso E Super, Haitian vetiver, white musk (nose: Francois Demachy).
- Light Blue: the one used in a past email is discontinued; use Light Blue EDT 2025 or Light Blue EDP in future.
- Never show sale prices as regular prices. "2ml starts at $7" was his line in Sep (site now shows $5.99: recheck before use).
- Title and bottle must always match; use forward-facing product photos; AI images that look artificial or fake get rejected.
- Favorites he called out: Tobacco Vanille, Tom Ford Oud Wood, Prada Paradigme, Azzaro The Most Wanted (Parfum / EDP Intense), Dior Homme 2020, Acqua Di Gio Parfum 2024, JPG Paradise Garden (vacation), Creed Virgin Island Water.
