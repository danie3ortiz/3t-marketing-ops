Sources: "Scent Pilot Cluade Project Info" Google Doc (export of the Claude Project, compiled 2026-10-30, facts verified 2026-10-29 unless marked), Figma sent emails, Klaviyo. Anything marked UNVERIFIED must not be stated as fact in copy.

## Positioning
Online retailer of designer and niche fragrances, sold as decants/samples and full bottles so shoppers can try before committing. Homepage headline: "Testing Before Investing."
- Founder-stated purpose: a trusted source to buy fragrance without committing to a full bottle.
- Differentiation: trusted guidance plus convenient sampling. Small sizes alone are not exclusive to this store.
- Hero attribute: low-commitment sampling before a full bottle.
- UNVERIFIED: competitor set; primary market assumed US.

## Customer archetype (hypothesis, not analytics)
Founder-stated: US, male, 18-44. Primary: the cautious explorer who watches reviews but wants to test before buying a full bottle. Secondary: fragrance hobbyists seeking variety; shoppers wanting portable sizes.
- Watches fragrance YouTubers (Gents Scents, Aromatix, Fragrance Flan, FB Fragrance, Cal Cologne); shops designer (Sephora, Macy's) and niche (Bloomingdale's).
- Fears / deal-killers: fakes, diluted fragrances, poor customer service, incorrect product descriptions, price, performance, no compliments.
- Do not use occupation, income, marital status or children as targeting requirements.

## Brand-wide constraints
- No exclamation marks, no emoji.
- Do not imply a fragrance guarantees attraction, respect or compliments.
- Do not promise refunds for disliking a scent. The guarantee covers authenticity only. Return policy: all sales final.
- Do not describe products as manufacturer-packaged or licensed. The bottle is Scent Pilot's own, the liquid is from the original manufacturer.
- Do not claim lower cost per ml than a full bottle without comparing actual prices.
- Do not promise shipping speed.
- Urgency or scarcity only when the offer or inventory supports it.
- Do not quote reviews without approval.
- Avoid "dupes" as a selling word (inferred caution, not a stated rule).
- No formal banned-word list exists yet (Danie to confirm).
