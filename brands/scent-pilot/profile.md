## Design style (observed, Figma)
Observed from the Sep 22 send "The Curated Way To Try 170+ Fragrances Without Losing Your Mind" (Figma node 8990:498, Revision 4). Exact hex codes and font names not extracted yet.
- 600px wide emails, built and sliced in Figma (designs are exported as image slices).
- Top bar: black, white uppercase text: "FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS OVER $50".
- Header: white band with centered SCENT PILOT logo.
- Body: dark navy / near-black background, white text throughout.
- Headlines: large, uppercase, monospace/typewriter-style font, letter-spaced.
- Body copy: small, clean sans-serif, centered or left in two-column blocks.
- Buttons: white rectangles, black uppercase text, square corners. Labels say the action ("FIND YOUR SCENT", "EXPLORE THE COLLECTION", "SHOP EROS EDP").
- Imagery: moody, dark product photography with spotlight lighting; bottles as hero.
- Recurring modules: hero with button; "Everywhere else vs Scent Pilot" comparison with a 2ml vial image; "This week's pick" fragrance-note diagram (Top / Heart / Base callouts); 2-up alternating product image + text cards with buttons; "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" close.
- Footer: white, logo, two outlined buttons ("Fragrance", "New Arrivals"), "(c) 2026, Scentpilot".
